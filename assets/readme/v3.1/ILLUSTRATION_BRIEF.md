# ToolKnit 3.1 comic illustration brief

## Shared direction

Eight wide 16:9 black-and-white scenes, exported at 2048 x 1152. Sparse uneven
pen linework, restrained hatching, pure-white backgrounds and generous space.
Reference FH-023 (Deadpan Doodle) for drawing language only; do not reproduce
its characters, props, scene, composition or text.

Recurring characters: a short-black-haired boy developer in a plain shirt,
calm expression, and one small black spider companion with white eyes per
moment. The spider sits on his head and can reach objects with a long leg.
Use the approved opening image for identity. Keep dialogue short, legible
and contained within roomy speech bubbles with clear speaker tails.

## Refinement and export

The recording and file scenes initially contained an extra spider within a
moment. Targeted image edits removed the extra character while preserving
the remaining composition, text and linework. Final exports are downsampled
to 2K, converted to grayscale and encoded as lossless WebP; screenshots are
actual captures handled separately. See [the asset manifest](../README.md).
